Most software companies grow their public surface as they grow their product line. More products mean more pages, more navigation, more things competing for attention.

We try to do the opposite.

Eclectic Byte is a small studio, and each product starts with a narrow problem: a habit app that helps you build routines without turning progress into another chore, a reporting tool that turns Shopify data into VAT working papers you can actually review, an integration that keeps Shopify and bol.com operations in sync.

The products are different, but the approach is the same. Pick a problem that is specific enough to solve well. Keep the interface quiet. Make the supporting documentation easy to find when someone needs it.

That is also why this site is deliberately small. The homepage shows the commercial products, the legal pages cover the details that need to be public, and the blog holds the longer notes that do not belong in a product surface.

We will use this space to write about product decisions, the trade-offs behind the tools, and the parts of building software that are easy to skip over. No release cadence promises, no growth hacks, just notes from the studio.
